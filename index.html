🔥 Prometheus Fire Predictor

We have visited many wildfire-prone areas and witnessed the devastating effects of climate change. Wildfires cannot always be controlled once they start, but if we get information earlier, we can prevent them. That’s why we made Prometheus — a device that predicts fire risks using sensors for temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and smoke, providing real-time alerts to prevent disasters.
