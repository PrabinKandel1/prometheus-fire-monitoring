🚀 What is Prometheus?

Prometheus is a technology-based fire risk prediction and monitoring system. It combines environmental sensor data, real-time monitoring, data visualization and prediction technologies to provide useful information about possible fire risks.

🌡️

Smart Monitoring

Monitors important environmental conditions such as temperature, humidity, smoke and soil-related data.

📊

Data Analysis

Presents collected information through clear graphs and visual data to make monitoring easier.

🔥

Fire Prediction

Uses available sensor information to help identify conditions associated with potential fire risk.

🎯 Our Mission

Technology for a Safer Future

Our mission is to explore how modern technology can be used to improve fire awareness, monitoring and early risk detection. Prometheus aims to turn environmental data into meaningful information that can support faster awareness and better decision-making.
